#### **3. Typography**

The brand uses a pairing of a geometric sans-serif and a classic serif.

* 
**Primary Typeface (Sans-Serif):** **Manrope** 


* *Usage:* Headlines, UI elements, modern/clean text application.


* 
**Secondary Typeface (Serif):** **Adobe Garamond Pro** 


* *Usage:* Body copy, formal correspondence, elegance markers.
